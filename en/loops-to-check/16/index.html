These loops are not tracks: they are candidates. A sweep compared the running loops mapped in OpenStreetMap with the facilities the French ministry lists, and these appear nowhere. But a loop can be a karting circuit, a BMX track, a horse-training ring, or the trace of something long gone. Nobody has been there.

You know the place? Your answer settles it.
