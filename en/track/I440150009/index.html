College Prive Saint Laurent

3 rue jean XXIII, 44130 Blain · Loire-Atlantique

College Prive Saint Laurent is an athletics venue in Blain (Loire-Atlantique), Pays de la Loire, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue is open to the public at set times.
