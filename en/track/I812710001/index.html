Complexe de Moletrincade

Rue du Gendarme Godefroid Moletrincade, 81370 Saint-Sulpice-la-Pointe · Tarn

Complexe de Moletrincade is an athletics venue in Saint-Sulpice-la-Pointe (Tarn), Occitanie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. Access is free and unrestricted.
