College Jean Jaures

55 Boulevard Bertolucci, 13600 La Ciotat · Bouches-du-Rhône

College Jean Jaures is an athletics venue in La Ciotat (Bouches-du-Rhône), Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides showers.
