Lycee Alain Fournier

50 Rue Stéphane Mallarmé, 18000 Bourges · Cher

Lycee Alain Fournier is an athletics venue in Bourges (Cher), Centre-Val de Loire, France. It has a 400 m cinder / gravel running track. It also offers shot put. The venue is open to the public at set times.
