Parc des Hures

136 r edmond de palezieux, 62224 Équihen-Plage · Pas-de-Calais

Parc des Hures is an athletics venue in Équihen-Plage (Pas-de-Calais), Hauts-de-France, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
