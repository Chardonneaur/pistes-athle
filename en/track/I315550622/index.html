École maternelle Jules Ferry

8 Avenue Jules Ferry, 31200 Toulouse · Haute-Garonne

École maternelle Jules Ferry is an athletics venue in Toulouse (Haute-Garonne), Occitanie, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track with 3 lanes. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides floodlighting. The venue is open to the public at set times.
