Aire Sportive

28 Route de Louroux, 37800 Sainte-Maure-de-Touraine · Indre-et-Loire

Aire Sportive is an athletics venue in Sainte-Maure-de-Touraine (Indre-et-Loire), Centre-Val de Loire, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. It also offers long jump pit and shot put. Access is free and unrestricted.
