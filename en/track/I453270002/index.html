College de la Foret

Rue de l'Orme Tiseau, 45470 Traînou · Loiret

College de la Foret is an athletics venue in Traînou (Loiret), Centre-Val de Loire, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. It also offers long jump pit. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
