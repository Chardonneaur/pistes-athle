College Emile Verhaeren

108 Boulevard de la Republique, 92210 Saint-Cloud · Hauts-de-Seine

College Emile Verhaeren is an athletics venue in Saint-Cloud (Hauts-de-Seine), Île-de-France, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides changing rooms and showers.
