Collège Louis Pasteur

7 rue du Dr. fritsch Sermaize les bains, 51250 Sermaize-les-Bains · Marne

Collège Louis Pasteur is an athletics venue in Sermaize-les-Bains (Marne), Grand Est, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides floodlighting and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
