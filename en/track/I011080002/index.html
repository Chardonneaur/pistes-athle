Gymnase Municipal

Champel, 01270 Coligny · Ain

Gymnase Municipal is an athletics venue in Coligny (Ain), Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France. It has a 150 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting.
