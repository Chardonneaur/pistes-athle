Complexe Sportif Montbauron

24 allée pierre de coubertin, 78000 Versailles · Yvelines

Complexe Sportif Montbauron is an athletics venue in Versailles (Yvelines), Île-de-France, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track with 8 lanes. It also offers long jump pit. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
