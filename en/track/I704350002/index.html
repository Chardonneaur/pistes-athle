Complexe sportif

Rue du Stade, 70280 Raddon-et-Chapendu · Haute-Saône

Complexe sportif is an athletics venue in Raddon-et-Chapendu (Haute-Saône), Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, France. It has an running track. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere.
