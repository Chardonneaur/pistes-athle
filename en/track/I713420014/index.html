Collège René Cassin

3 Rue du 8 Mai, 71600 Paray-le-Monial · Saône-et-Loire

Collège René Cassin is an athletics venue in Paray-le-Monial (Saône-et-Loire), Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere.
