Complexe sportif du collège Maurice Fombeurre

1 Rue de la Rousselinière, 79340 Ménigoute · Deux-Sèvres

Complexe sportif du collège Maurice Fombeurre is an athletics venue in Ménigoute (Deux-Sèvres), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere.
