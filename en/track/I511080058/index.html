City Stade Branly

Rue Edouard Branly, 51000 Châlons-en-Champagne · Marne

City Stade Branly is an athletics venue in Châlons-en-Champagne (Marne), Grand Est, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
