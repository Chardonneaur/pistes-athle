Complexe Sportif Marc Heron

23 Rue Jean Jaurès, 28700 Auneau-Bleury-Saint-Symphorien · Eure-et-Loir

Complexe Sportif Marc Heron is an athletics venue in Auneau-Bleury-Saint-Symphorien (Eure-et-Loir), Centre-Val de Loire, France. It has a 400 m cinder / gravel running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
