Lycée Climatique René Billères

6 Avenue Marcel Lemettre, 65400 Argelès-Gazost · Hautes-Pyrénées

Lycée Climatique René Billères is an athletics venue in Argelès-Gazost (Hautes-Pyrénées), Occitanie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. It also offers shot put.
