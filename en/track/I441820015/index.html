Complexe de la Viauderie

Rue de la Viauderie, 44730 Saint-Michel-Chef-Chef · Loire-Atlantique

Complexe de la Viauderie is an athletics venue in Saint-Michel-Chef-Chef (Loire-Atlantique), Pays de la Loire, France. It has a 200 m cinder / gravel running track. It also offers long jump pit and shot put. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers.
