Stade Municipal

Rue Alphée Mazieras, 24000 Périgueux · Dordogne

Stade Municipal is an athletics venue in Périgueux (Dordogne), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
