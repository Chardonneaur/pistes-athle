Complexe Sportif de la Grangette

Avenue de Geneve, 74200 Thonon-les-Bains · Haute-Savoie

Complexe Sportif de la Grangette is an athletics venue in Thonon-les-Bains (Haute-Savoie), Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track with 6 lanes. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
