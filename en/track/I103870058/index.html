Complexe multi-activités des blossieres

19 Rue Charles Tanret, 10000 Troyes · Département non renseigné

Complexe multi-activités des blossieres is an athletics venue in Troyes (Département non renseigné), France. It has a 225 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers.
