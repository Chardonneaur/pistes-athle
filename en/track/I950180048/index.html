Complexe Sportif Pierre de Coubertin

2 Rue des Cevennes, 95100 Argenteuil · Val-d'Oise

Complexe Sportif Pierre de Coubertin is an athletics venue in Argenteuil (Val-d'Oise), Île-de-France, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. It also offers shot put. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
