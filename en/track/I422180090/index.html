Parc des sports de l'Etivallière

5 Allée des Frères Gauthier, 42000 Saint-Étienne · Loire

Parc des sports de l'Etivallière is an athletics venue in Saint-Étienne (Loire), Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. Access is free and unrestricted.
