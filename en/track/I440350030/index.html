Complexe Sportif Bourgoin Decombes

3 rue louis maisonneuve, 44240 La Chapelle-sur-Erdre · Loire-Atlantique

Complexe Sportif Bourgoin Decombes is an athletics venue in La Chapelle-sur-Erdre (Loire-Atlantique), Pays de la Loire, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. Access is free and unrestricted.
