Complexe sportif de Caqueret

Rue de Caqueret, 58300 Decize · Nièvre

Complexe sportif de Caqueret is an athletics venue in Decize (Nièvre), Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. The venue provides changing rooms and showers.
