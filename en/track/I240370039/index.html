lycée des métiers sud Périgord Hélène Duc

35 boulevard Chanzy, 24100 Bergerac · Dordogne

lycée des métiers sud Périgord Hélène Duc is an athletics venue in Bergerac (Dordogne), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium.
