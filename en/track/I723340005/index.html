Cité scolaire Paul Scarron

Route de Mont Saint Jean La Croix, 72140 Sillé-le-Guillaume · Sarthe

Cité scolaire Paul Scarron is an athletics venue in Sillé-le-Guillaume (Sarthe), Pays de la Loire, France. It has a 310 m cinder / gravel running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers.
