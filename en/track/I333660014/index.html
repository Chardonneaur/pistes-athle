Plaine des Sports Laurent Ricci

Route du Bouilh, 33240 Saint-André-de-Cubzac · Gironde

Plaine des Sports Laurent Ricci is an athletics venue in Saint-André-de-Cubzac (Gironde), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track with 8 lanes. It also offers long jump pit, triple jump, high jump area, pole vault, shot put, discus, hammer throw and javelin. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
