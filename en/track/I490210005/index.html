Stade Roger Serreau

Boulevard de la prévoté, 49250 Beaufort-en-Anjou · Maine-et-Loire

Stade Roger Serreau is an athletics venue in Beaufort-en-Anjou (Maine-et-Loire), Pays de la Loire, France. It has a 395 m cinder / gravel running track. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
