complexe multisports

Rue Pierre et Marie Curie, 35150 Janzé · Ille-et-Vilaine

complexe multisports is an athletics venue in Janzé (Ille-et-Vilaine), Bretagne, France. It has an running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere.
