Complexe EuroMéditerranéen

rte de vimenet, 48100 Montrodat · Lozère

Complexe EuroMéditerranéen is an athletics venue in Montrodat (Lozère), Occitanie, France. It has an running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere.
