Ensemble sportif de Népoui

Népoui, 98827 Poya · Nouvelle-Calédonie

Ensemble sportif de Népoui is an athletics venue in Poya (Nouvelle-Calédonie), Nouvelle-Calédonie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides showers. Access is free and unrestricted.
