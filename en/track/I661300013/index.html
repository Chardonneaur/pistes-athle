Plaine de Loisirs

Rue du Lac, 66340 Osséja · Pyrénées-Orientales

Plaine de Loisirs is an athletics venue in Osséja (Pyrénées-Orientales), Occitanie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. Access is free and unrestricted.
