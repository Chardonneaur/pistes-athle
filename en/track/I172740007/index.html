Complexe sportif

Rue Saint Rogatien, 17180 Périgny · Charente-Maritime

Complexe sportif is an athletics venue in Périgny (Charente-Maritime), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
