Complexe Bruno Benedetti

Rue du Moulin des Hayes BOURGTHEROULDE-INFREVILLE, 27520 Grand Bourgtheroulde · Eure

Complexe Bruno Benedetti is an athletics venue in Grand Bourgtheroulde (Eure), Normandie, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue is open to the public at set times.
