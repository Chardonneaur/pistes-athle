Terrain des Sports

Route de Pelice - Cherreau, 72400 Cherré-Au · Sarthe · Cherreau

Terrain des Sports is an athletics venue in Cherré-Au (Sarthe), Pays de la Loire, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a 2 to 4 lane athletics track, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
