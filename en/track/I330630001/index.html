Stade Galin

Rue Galin, 33000 Bordeaux · Gironde

Stade Galin is an athletics venue in Bordeaux (Gironde), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. It also offers high jump area. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
