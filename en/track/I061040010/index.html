Parc Sportif et Paysager

esplanade du bord de mer, 06190 Roquebrune-Cap-Martin · Alpes-Maritimes

Parc Sportif et Paysager is an athletics venue in Roquebrune-Cap-Martin (Alpes-Maritimes), Provence-Alpes-Côte d'Azur, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides floodlighting. Access is free and unrestricted.
