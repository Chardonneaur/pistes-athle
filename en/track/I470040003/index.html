Stade Louis Jamet

Avenue Jean François-Poncet, 47190 Aiguillon · Lot-et-Garonne

Stade Louis Jamet is an athletics venue in Aiguillon (Lot-et-Garonne), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has a 380 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers.
