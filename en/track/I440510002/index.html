Lycee Agricole

29 Rue de Rennes, 44590 Derval · Loire-Atlantique

Lycee Agricole is an athletics venue in Derval (Loire-Atlantique), Pays de la Loire, France. It has a 400 m cinder / gravel running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
