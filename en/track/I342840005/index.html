Petit Stade de Ponderach

Voie Verte, 34220 Saint-Pons-de-Thomières · Hérault

Petit Stade de Ponderach is an athletics venue in Saint-Pons-de-Thomières (Hérault), Occitanie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. It also offers long jump pit. The venue provides changing rooms and showers.
