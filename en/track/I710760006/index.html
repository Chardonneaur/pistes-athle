Stade Garibaldi

Rue Garibaldi, 71100 Chalon-sur-Saône · Saône-et-Loire

Stade Garibaldi is an athletics venue in Chalon-sur-Saône (Saône-et-Loire), Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, France. The venue provides changing rooms and showers.
