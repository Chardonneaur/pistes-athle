stade de boulevard du 8 mai

Boulevard du 8 mai 1945, 59220 Denain · Nord

stade de boulevard du 8 mai is an athletics venue in Denain (Nord), Hauts-de-France, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. It also offers long jump pit. Access is free and unrestricted.
