Essa

331 Avenue Général de Gaulle, 69500 Bron · Rhône

Essa is an athletics venue in Bron (Rhône), Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
