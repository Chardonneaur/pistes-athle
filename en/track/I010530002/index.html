Complexe Sportif de la Jeunesse Laique

42 Rue Charles Robin, 01000 Bourg-en-Bresse · Ain

Complexe Sportif de la Jeunesse Laique is an athletics venue in Bourg-en-Bresse (Ain), Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
