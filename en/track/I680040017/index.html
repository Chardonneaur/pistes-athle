Piste Finlandaise

Rue de la Foret, 68130 Altkirch · Haut-Rhin

Piste Finlandaise is an athletics venue in Altkirch (Haut-Rhin), Grand Est, France. It has an natural surface running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
