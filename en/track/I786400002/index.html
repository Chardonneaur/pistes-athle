Centre Omnisports R. Wagner

17 avenue robert wagner, 78140 Vélizy-Villacoublay · Yvelines

Centre Omnisports R. Wagner is an athletics venue in Vélizy-Villacoublay (Yvelines), Île-de-France, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
