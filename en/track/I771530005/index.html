Complexe Sportif Jesse Owens

College de l'Europe, 77230 Dammartin-en-Goële · Seine-et-Marne

Complexe Sportif Jesse Owens is an athletics venue in Dammartin-en-Goële (Seine-et-Marne), Île-de-France, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
