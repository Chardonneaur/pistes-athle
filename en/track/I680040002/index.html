Terrain de Sport ban communal de Carspach

30 Route de Carspach, 68130 Altkirch · Haut-Rhin

Terrain de Sport ban communal de Carspach is an athletics venue in Altkirch (Haut-Rhin), Grand Est, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium. The venue provides floodlighting. Access is free and unrestricted.
