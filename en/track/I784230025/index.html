Institut Culturel Franco Japonais

7 Rue Jean-Pierre Timbaud, 78180 Montigny-le-Bretonneux · Yvelines

Institut Culturel Franco Japonais is an athletics venue in Montigny-le-Bretonneux (Yvelines), Île-de-France, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
