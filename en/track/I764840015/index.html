Espace sportif Jean Jaurès

Rue de Picardie OISSEL, 76350 Oissel · Seine-Maritime

Espace sportif Jean Jaurès is an athletics venue in Oissel (Seine-Maritime), Normandie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. Access is free and unrestricted.
