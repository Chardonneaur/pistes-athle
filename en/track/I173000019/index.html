Collège MENDES FRANCE

Avenue de Lisbonne MIREUIL, 17000 La Rochelle · Charente-Maritime

Collège MENDES FRANCE is an athletics venue in La Rochelle (Charente-Maritime), Nouvelle-Aquitaine, France. It has an asphalt / tarmac running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue is open to the public at set times.
