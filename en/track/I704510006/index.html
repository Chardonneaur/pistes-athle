Stade Ludovic Octave FROSSARD

2 Rue du Stade, 70250 Ronchamp · Haute-Saône

Stade Ludovic Octave FROSSARD is an athletics venue in Ronchamp (Haute-Saône), Bourgogne-Franche-Comté, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track.
