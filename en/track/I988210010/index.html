Ensemble sportif municipal de Païta

Boulevard de l'Arène, 98840 Païta · Nouvelle-Calédonie

Ensemble sportif municipal de Païta is an athletics venue in Païta (Nouvelle-Calédonie), Nouvelle-Calédonie, France. It has an cinder / gravel running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium, and its lap length is stated nowhere. The venue provides changing rooms and showers.
