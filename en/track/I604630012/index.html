Complexe sportif Georges Lenne

150 Avenue de l'Europe, 60180 Nogent-sur-Oise · Oise

Complexe sportif Georges Lenne is an athletics venue in Nogent-sur-Oise (Oise), Hauts-de-France, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
