Stade du Sibano

Avenue des Moulineaux, 78590 Noisy-le-Roi · Yvelines

Stade du Sibano is an athletics venue in Noisy-le-Roi (Yvelines), Île-de-France, France. It has a 400 m synthetic (tartan) running track. The venue provides changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
