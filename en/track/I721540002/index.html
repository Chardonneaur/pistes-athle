Prytanée national militaire Quartier Henri IV

22 Rue du Collège, 72200 La Flèche · Sarthe

Prytanée national militaire Quartier Henri IV is an athletics venue in La Flèche (Sarthe), Pays de la Loire, France. It has an synthetic (tartan) running track. The ministry's register classes it as a standalone track: it is not part of an athletics stadium.
