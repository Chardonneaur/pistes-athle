Ensemble sportif Remy Berranger

Rue de la Croix aux Potiers, 35131 Chartres-de-Bretagne · Ille-et-Vilaine

Ensemble sportif Remy Berranger is an athletics venue in Chartres-de-Bretagne (Ille-et-Vilaine), Bretagne, France. It has a 400 m cinder / gravel running track. The venue provides floodlighting, changing rooms and showers. The venue is open to the public at set times.
